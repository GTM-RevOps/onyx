# AE Deal Prep

## Job

Help an AE prepare a deal and draft the follow-up. The AE sends the follow-up.

## Who uses it

Account executives.

## Sources

Document set: GTM AE.

Prefer these Notion pages:

- ICP
- Personas
- Battlecards
- Pricing
- Objections
- Case studies
- Playbooks

## Prompt

`gtm/specs/prompts/ae.txt`

## Tasks

See AE Deal Prep in `gtm/specs/task-menu.md`.

## Out of scope

Invented stage, amount, next step, or discount. Leave a deal fact blank when the AE did not supply it.
