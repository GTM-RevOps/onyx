# RevOps

## Job

Help RevOps check definitions and draft a Notion update. A human publishes the update.

## Who uses it

RevOps.

## Sources

Document set: GTM RevOps. The page list is the whole GTM space.

Prefer these Notion pages:

- ICP
- Personas
- Messaging
- Battlecards
- Pricing
- Objections
- Playbooks
- Case studies
- Release notes

## Prompt

`gtm/specs/prompts/revops.txt`

## Tasks

See RevOps in `gtm/specs/task-menu.md`.

## Out of scope

Publishing a page, changing a CRM field, and adding a definition the pages do not contain.
