# SDR Helper

## Job

Help an SDR decide if an account fits and draft the first touch. The SDR sends the message.

## Who uses it

SDRs and SDR managers.

## Sources

Document set: GTM SDR.

Prefer these Notion pages:

- ICP
- Personas
- Messaging
- Objections
- Playbooks

## Prompt

`gtm/specs/prompts/sdr.txt`

The bootstrap adds `gtm/specs/prompts/harness.txt` in front of that file.

## Tasks

See SDR Helper in `gtm/specs/task-menu.md`.

## Out of scope

Pricing discounts, legal terms, and anything the SDR would send without reading.
