# Notion space

Create one Notion team space named **PushPress GTM**. Share it with the Notion integration whose token is `NOTION_INTEGRATION_TOKEN`. Put the space URL or page id in `NOTION_ROOT_PAGE_ID` so the connector stays inside this space.

Give each topic its own page. Use the headings below. Do not paste customer-specific prices or legal terms into a page a whole company can read.

```
PushPress GTM
  ICP
  Personas
  Messaging
  Battlecards
  Pricing
  Objections
  Playbooks
    SDR
    AE
    CS
    Manager
  Case studies
  Release notes
```

## ICP

- Firmographics
- Must-have
- Disqualifiers
- Segments

## Personas

One child page per buyer. Use the same headings on each page.

- Role
- Jobs to be done
- Pains
- What they need to hear
- Who else is in the deal

## Messaging

- Category line
- Problem
- Promise
- Proof points
- Words we use
- Words we avoid

## Battlecards

One child page per competitor.

- Their claim
- Our answer
- Proof
- Question to ask
- Landmines

## Pricing

- Packages
- What is included
- Rules a rep must follow
- What a rep must not offer

## Objections

One block per objection.

- They say
- We answer
- Proof
- Role that hears it

## Playbooks

Keep SDR, AE, CS, and Manager as child pages.

- Goal of the motion
- Steps
- Required questions
- Definition of done
- Handoff

## Case studies

One child page per story you can share.

- Customer type
- Problem
- What they used
- Outcome
- What a rep may quote

## Release notes

One child page per release. Newest first.

- Date
- What shipped
- Who it is for
- What we do not claim yet
